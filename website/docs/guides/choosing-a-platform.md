# Choosing a Platform

## Decision factors

- **Audience & discovery** — built-in networks (e.g. Substack) vs owned domain.
- **Monetization** — paid subs, sponsorships, or both.
- **Deliverability & domain** — custom sending domain, authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC).
- **Design control** — hosted templates vs HTML/MJML pipelines.
- **Exports** — can you leave with your list and archives?

See also the Platforms section in the README (when merged) and [Ops](/docs/ops).
