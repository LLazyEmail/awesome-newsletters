# Newsletter directories

Curated “best of” lists and directories of newsletters to subscribe to or study.

- [eInvestigator: List of the best email newsletters](https://www.einvestigator.com/list-of-the-best-email-newsletters/) — long-running roundup of notable email newsletters
- [Inc: Best Newsletters 2020](https://www.inc.com/jessica-stillman/best-newsletters-2020.html) — Inc magazine picks from 2020 (historical snapshot)
- [InsideHook: Best single-operator email newsletters](https://www.insidehook.com/feature/internet/best-single-operator-email-newsletters-internet) — profiles of one-person newsletter operations
- [Letterlist](https://letterlist.com/) — searchable directory of newsletters across topics
- [Local News Lab: Email newsletters reading list](https://localnewslab.org/guide/newsletters/appendix-one-email-newsletters-reading-list/) — journalism-focused newsletter reading list
- [Luminary Labs: 200 newsletters (crowdsourced)](https://www.luminary-labs.com/insight/200-newsletters-a-crowdsourced-list-of-must-read-emails/) — crowdsourced “must-read” newsletter compilation
- [Supply Chain Digital: Top 10 supply chain newsletters](https://supplychaindigital.com/top10/top-10-supply-chain-newsletters) — vertical shortlist for supply-chain readers
- [The Coolist: Best newsletters](https://www.thecoolist.com/best-newsletters/) — general “best newsletters” roundup article
- [zudochkin/awesome-newsletters](https://github.com/zudochkin/awesome-newsletters) — categorized developer & tech newsletters (**related awesome list**, not a duplicate of this repo’s focus on newsletter craft/resources)
